2026 Revenue Tracker

A personal finance tracker that runs entirely in your browser. Record expected and actual revenue, set up monthly recurring income, and track VAT, profit tax and salaries in Caribbean guilders or US dollars. Compare your budget against actuals, then review your profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow reports. All data stays on your device in IndexedDB, and you can export or import a JSON backup at any time.
